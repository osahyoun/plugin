# Ekō Microsite Brand Guidelines

## Minimum rules

- Show the approved Ekō logo in the header and footer.
- Do not redraw, recolour, stretch, crop, rotate, or decorate the logo.
- Keep clear space around the mark and provide useful `alt` text.
- Use one visual system from hero through donation and footer.
- Keep the main action visually dominant.
- Use the translated Ekō Home, About, Privacy, and Contact links in the footer.
- Keep approved Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, and Facebook links working; do not use
  placeholder `#` destinations.
- Give campaign images meaningful alt text. Decorative treatment must not hide the only
  accessible description of the hero image.

## Reference palette

The bundled template follows the current Pronto campaign UI tokens:

- Purple: `#6400ff`
- Mint: `#28dc87`
- Ink: `#101820`
- White: `#ffffff`
- Soft neutral: `#f4f4f5`
- Error: `#b0131d`

Purple is the primary action colour. Mint is an accent and success colour. Maintain at
least WCAG AA contrast for normal text and visible keyboard focus.

## Type and layout

Pronto uses Poppins. The standalone template uses a dependency-free system fallback so
it remains fast and self-contained; a licensed/approved Poppins webfont may replace it
at deployment. Use short paragraphs, large campaign headlines, generous spacing, and a
single-column action card on mobile.

## Logo provenance

`assets/microsite-template/public/assets/eko-logo-purple.svg` is the same approved
purple Ekō asset referenced by Pronto:

`https://champaign-assets-production.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/unique/Eko_Logo_Purple.svg`

If the organisation supplies a newer brand kit, replace the bundled file without
changing its proportions and update this reference.

Prosecco may use the approved white logo on its purple header/footer and the purple logo
on light surfaces. The plain text `Ekō` wordmark is not an adequate production
replacement when the approved asset is available.

## Content and imagery

- The general fast petition layout must remain topic-neutral. Do not ship dolphin,
  ocean, or other campaign-specific claims as generic template copy.
- A specialist story layout may contain campaign-specific structure only when every
  fact and image is replaced with approved campaign content before launch.
- Prefer campaigner-uploaded imagery or existing Prosecco assets. Unsplash is an option,
  not a dependency.
- Record source/licence provenance and test the crop on small screens.
