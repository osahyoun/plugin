## Why This Is Useful

- Fast crisis hook in the first sentence.
- Converts a broad technology issue into a concrete decision point for named companies.
- Uses insider/worker resistance as proof that the ask is timely and credible.
- Repeats short CTA beats in plain language.
- Keeps the pressure target visible throughout.

## Reusable Patterns

- Open with a clear threat plus why it matters now.
- Show that a strategic opening exists.
- Name the target group and the exact behavior being demanded.
- Use a short standalone CTA line early.
- Close with member solidarity and the power of collective backing.
