## Why This Is Useful

- Strong example of rapid-response action copy.
- The subject line signals immediacy without explaining everything.
- Opens with live events and a concrete location.
- Makes the member action very practical: call now.
- Uses a named worker story to humanize the ask.
- Reinforces coalition context and time pressure before repeating the CTA.

## Reusable Patterns

- Lead with what is happening right now.
- Explain why member action matters today.
- Give the practical instruction in plain language.
- Use one named person to make the stakes concrete.
- Repeat the call-in instruction near the close.
